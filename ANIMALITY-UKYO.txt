UKYO: SWAN ANIMALITY, first playable version

Run Play-FBNeo.cmd. Load ultimate.lua in FBNeo's Lua Script window and click
Run. The console should say "Ukyo Swan animality ready". Reload the script
if it was already running before these files were installed.

Trigger: Ukyo wins with the game's native blood-fountain fatality. Either
player can be Ukyo. Ordinary hit blood, normal knockouts and bisections do
not trigger it. This first version follows the game's fatality selection;
there is no new animality input or change to its random fatality selection.
The existing F ultimate button and steady translucent shadows are included.

COSTUME PALETTES
The human and swan automatically use Ukyo's selected costume palette for
either player. Crest, forelock, sash and tail follow his outfit colors.
All 19 original human colors map exactly to native palette entries; the
additional swan shades map to the closest corresponding native shade.
Palette selection is logged when the animality starts. Costume colors are
read from the game's selected ROM palettes, avoiding temporary hit/POW
flashes. Recolored frames are cached; transparency and sprite alignment
are preserved. Reload the Lua script after installing this update.
Preview: assets/ukyo-animality/costume-palettes-preview.png

LOGGING
Selecting animality.lua directly in FBNeo also starts ultimate.lua and all
its features. Both entry files now show startup messages in the Lua console.
Reload ultimate.lua to enable logging. Timestamped messages appear in the
FBNeo Lua console and are appended to animality.log next to ultimate.lua.
Logs include loading, each KO (with character, HP and native state), skipped
fountain triggers with a reason, animality start, impact, victory and any
cancellation or timeout. No messages are written on every animation frame.
The required victim state is 0318 with HP=0 and living Ukyo as the opponent.

This version needs FBNeo and the running Lua script. Its custom graphics
are loaded by Lua; the victim's fountain and Ukyo's final victory pose are
native game animations. HBMAME's normal Play.cmd retains F and the shadow
fix, but does not render this FBNeo animality overlay.

DESIGN
The human at the start and end uses Ukyo's actual game sprite, extracted
from a palette-isolated emulator capture. The swan has his cobalt-blue
swept-back crest, hanging forelock, blue breast band, tan sword-grip accent,
and three blue tail feathers suggesting the folds of his hakama. Its white
feathers use Ukyo's kimono colors. The beak has a sharp blade-like silhouette.

REEL (16 drawings, numbered 00-15 in the asset folder)
00: Native Ukyo stance; 8 game frames.
01: Ukyo folds low into feathered sleeves; 12 frames.
02: Compact hybrid; blue hair becomes the swan's crest; 12 frames.
03: Swan with folded wings and an S-shaped neck; 12 frames.
04: Low launch preparation; 10 frames.
05: Wings rise; 10 frames on launch, then reused for wingbeats.
06: Downstroke and lift; 10 frames, then reused for wingbeats.
07: Horizontal glide; alternates with 05/06 while approaching the victim.
08: Beak thrust into the victim's torso; 7 frames, synchronized to the
    native fountain emitter appearing.
09: Neck recoils and wings rise; 9 frames.
10: Wings brake while moving back; 9 frames.
11: Feet lower for landing; 9 frames.
12: Folded-wing landing hold while the native fountain finishes; 34 frames.
13: Reverse of 02, keeping identical anatomy; 12 frames.
14: Reverse of 01, feathers retract into Ukyo's sleeves; 12 frames.
15: Native human stance; 8 frames, followed by native Ukyo victory state 0600.

The time before impact adapts to the game's own hit-stop and fountain
animation. The reel uses a shared palette and a fixed origin in 128x128
cells. Mirrored versions and five camera scales are generated in advance.
The swan rises to torso height; its ground shadow follows the movement.

Ukyo capture as reference. The exact generation prompt is stored beside it.
